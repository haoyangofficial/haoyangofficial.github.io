Remote sensing change detection (RSCD) plays a crucial role in fields such as environmental monitoring and urban planning. In recent years, with the rise of foundational vision-language models like CLIP, there has been a growing trend of incorporating textual information into vision tasks. However, in the RSCD domain, few attempts have been made to leverage multimodal information effectively. Additionally, challenges remain in learning differential features and integrating data from multiple modalities. To address these issues, this study introduces AugCD, a novel model designed to enhance differential feature learning. We propose a pre-masking module that masks bi-temporal image pairs before generating text prompts, ensuring a more focused analysis of changes. Furthermore, we define a difference augment (DA) function to enhance differences in both visual and textual embeddings. To improve multimodal integration, we implement a deep fusion (DF) module, which fuses data across multiple layers, and enhance the Swin Transformer decoder with a U-Net structure to strengthen supervision of the fused embeddings. The proposed AugCD model achieves state-of-the-art (SOTA) performance on five public RSCD datasets: CDD, LEVIR-CD, LEVIR-CD+, SYSU-CD, and WHUCD.
